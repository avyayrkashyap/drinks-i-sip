About

A hot chocolate in the winter, a coffee in the summer, and a cocktail anytime is my favourite way to experience a new city. Moving to New York exposed me to the largest catalogue of these drinks, and as such, I wanted to find the best. This website is a running note of drinks I've sipped, and at times, chugged.

Feel free to drop me recommendations.

Methodology

I graded each hot chocolate along three criteria, each scored out of ten.

  • water_drop

    Cocoa presence

    How full is the taste of the cocoa? Is the bitterness controlled?

  • waves

    Body

    How close to just-right does the drink feel viscosity-wise?

  • hourglass_empty

    Sustainability

    How quickly does enjoyment of the drink decay?
